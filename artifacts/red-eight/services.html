01 · Custom builds

Built from
your brief.

Every custom build starts with a conversation — what you have, what you need, how the piece will be used and where it will live. We design in-house, build every component ourselves, and only use kiln-dried hardwood for load-bearing frames.

We work in oak, ash, walnut and maple. Upholstered components use the same lead upholsterers as our restoration work. Nothing goes through a sub-contractor.

  • Chairs, dining tables, banquettes and casegoods
  • Mortise & tenon and doweled joinery — no pocket screws
  • Custom dimensions, finishes and fabric/leather choices
  • Hospitality contract grades available
  • Stamped, numbered and warrantied

02 · Frame restoration

If the bones
are good.

A well-built antique frame is worth saving. We strip pieces to the bare wood, assess every joint, re-spring, re-web, and rebuild from the inside out. We tell you honestly what can be done — and what can't.

We work on chairs, sofas, club chairs, settees, and decorative seating from any era. Frames that are too far gone don't get patched; they get rebuilt or we'll say so.

  • Full structural assessment before work begins
  • Re-springing — eight-way hand-tied or sinuous wire
  • Re-webbing with jute and elasticated support
  • Joint repair, doweling, and frame consolidation
  • Wood finishing — stripped, stained and lacquered or oiled

03 · Reupholstery

Inside and
out.

We finish the inside the way we finish the outside. Hand-cut leather and fabric, hand-stitched detailing, properly tensioned and tacked. No staple guns on show work. Everything below the fabric matters to us.

We carry a curated range of full-grain leathers and natural-fibre fabrics, or we'll work with client-supplied material. Every hide is inspected before cutting.

  • Full-grain leather and natural-fibre fabric work
  • Hand-cut and hand-stitched details
  • Proper padding replacement — horsehair, foam, down
  • Contrast piping, tufting and button work
  • Client-supplied fabrics accepted

How it works

From first call
to delivery.

Every job follows the same process. You'll know where your piece is at every stage — we don't go quiet after the deposit.

Start your project
01
Week 1

Send us photos and measurements

Email us photos of the piece and its dimensions. We'll come back with an honest initial assessment — what can be saved, what needs to be rebuilt, and a rough cost range.

02
Week 1–2

In-shop assessment and quote

For restoration and reupholstery work, we'll ask you to bring the piece in. We strip it down, assess the frame and spring work, and give you a firm written quote within a week.

03
Week 2–3

Material and fabric selection

Choose from our full-grain leather and fabric range in the shop. We'll also work with client-supplied material. You'll see and touch every option before we cut anything.

04
Lead time: 30–90 days

Build or restoration work

We do the work on our shop floor — no sub-contractors, no off-site production. You'll get a progress update at the midpoint and when the piece is ready for final review.

05
Delivery

Delivery or collection

We offer white-glove delivery and installation across the GTA. Smaller pieces can be collected from the shop. Every piece leaves with a care card and our shop stamp.

Materials

We tell you what's underneath

Hardwood frames

Kiln-dried white oak, ash, walnut and maple. Mortise & tenon and pegged joinery. No MDF in load-bearing parts.

Full-grain leather

Hides sourced from three Canadian and European tanneries. Inspected on arrival. We don't use bonded or corrected-grain leather.

Natural-fibre fabrics

Mohair, linen, wool and contract-grade synthetic velvet. All fabrics tested for rub count before we offer them to clients.

Interior padding

Horsehair on traditional pieces, high-resilience foam on contemporary builds, down-blend on premium upholstery. No cheap polyester fill.
